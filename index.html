Problem Definition Framework

Who

Adult children (28–45) coordinating care for ageing parents (55+), and parents seeking generational clarity.

What

No single family member has a current, complete picture of what exists, where physical copies sit, or who the agents/nominees are.

When

Invisible in day-to-day life; acutely painful during sudden medical hospitalization, stroke, or bereavement.

Why It Matters

₹78,213 Crore sits completely unclaimed in RBI's Depositor Education and Awareness Fund (+26% YoY).

Why not a will-writing app? (Idea E Rejection)

Will-writing demands high emotional readiness and legal finality that Indian families resist on Day 1. SURELY instead starts with the neutral, non-threatening posture of a simple "where are the keys and passbooks" checklist, building trust before complex estate planning.

USER TESTING & VALIDATION

What We Learned From Real Families

We conducted qualitative prototype validation with 13 participants across India to stress-test our assumptions.

13
Participants Tested & Interviewed
11 of 13
Confirmed Personal Document Fragmentation
4.1 / 5
Average Anxiety & Severity Score
5.2
Institutions per Family on Average
01

What Was Confirmed

  • Pervasive Anxiety: 85% of adult children worry privately about finding health insurance and bank lockers during an emergency.
  • No Unified Single Record: Families have fragments in WhatsApp chats, notes apps, physical files, and memory.
02

What Was Challenged

  • Death is Not the Trigger: Framing around "after you pass away" triggers denial and reluctance. Framing around "twenty minutes of weekend organization" had 3x higher willingness.
  • No Real-Time Bank Sync: Users actively disliked linking net-banking credentials; manual entry of location was perceived as 10x safer.
03

What Surprised Us

  • Masking is the Trust Keystone: Parents loved the ability to hide the exact rupee amount while still letting children know the account exists.
  • Emergency Mode Was the "Aha" Feature: The high-contrast, uncluttered emergency screen made the utility immediately apparent.
04

What We Changed

  • Under 60s Single Screen Form: Replaced multi-step wizards with a single clean 5-field entry card that takes less than a minute.
  • Checklist Completeness Metaphor: Added the 8-category visual checklist to provide satisfying closure as items are filled.
